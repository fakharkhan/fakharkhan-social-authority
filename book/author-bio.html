About the Author

Fakhar Zaman Khan

Fakhar Zaman Khan is the Founder & CEO of Soft Pyramid LLC, a global software and automation consultancy founded in Lahore, Pakistan, in 2013. Under his leadership, Soft Pyramid has evolved from a small development studio into a recognized force in AI-powered automation, serving clients across North America, Europe, and the Middle East.

He is a verified creator in the n8n automation ecosystem, with global recognition for his AI workflows and automation frameworks. As a community leader, he has organized and hosted n8n Live events across Pakistan and the United States, democratizing access to automation education and empowering hundreds of developers and entrepreneurs.

Fakhar's expertise spans SaaS architecture, workflow automation, AI orchestration, and organizational leadership. He has mentored hundreds of developers and entrepreneurs, built automation ecosystems across MedTech, PropTech, and E-commerce, and spoken internationally about the intersection of AI and empathy.

His philosophy—that true innovation begins not in lines of code, but in lines of character—has shaped Soft Pyramid's culture and influenced a generation of technologists who believe in building beyond code.
