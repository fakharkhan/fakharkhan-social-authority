About the Book

A Journey from Lahore to Global Leadership

This book is the story of Soft Pyramid, a company that began in a single room in Lahore and grew into a global movement in AI-powered leadership. But more than that, it's the story of how empathy, trust, and learning became the real architectures of technology.

From that first email to international clients to where we stand today—spanning twelve years, six continents, and countless conversations—this journey includes failures that taught us more than successes, clients who became collaborators, and a team that transformed from employees into a community.

"The future of technology belongs not to those who can write the most elegant algorithms, but to those who can build the most trustworthy systems."

About the Author

Fakhar Zaman Khan

Founder & CEO of Soft Pyramid LLC, a global software and automation consultancy founded in Lahore, Pakistan, in 2013.

Under his leadership, Soft Pyramid has evolved from a small development studio into a recognized force in AI-powered automation, serving clients across North America, Europe, and the Middle East.

He is a verified creator in the n8n automation ecosystem, with global recognition for his AI workflows and automation frameworks. As a community leader, he has organized and hosted n8n Live events across Pakistan and the United States.

Fakhar's expertise spans SaaS architecture, workflow automation, AI orchestration, and organizational leadership. He has mentored hundreds of developers and entrepreneurs, built automation ecosystems across MedTech, PropTech, and E-commerce, and spoken internationally about the intersection of AI and empathy.
