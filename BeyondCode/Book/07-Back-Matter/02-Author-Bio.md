Under his leadership, Soft Pyramid has evolved from a small development studio into a recognized force in AI-powered automation, serving clients across North America, Europe, and the Middle East.

He has mentored hundreds of developers and entrepreneurs, built automation ecosystems across MedTech, PropTech, and E-commerce, and spoken internationally about the intersection of AI and empathy.

His philosophy—that true innovation begins not in lines of code, but in lines of character—has shaped Soft Pyramid's culture and influenced a generation of technologists who believe in building beyond code.
