Book Overview

Genre: Narrative Non-Fiction / Business Leadership / Technology & Innovation
Length: ~55,000–65,000 words
Tone: Reflective, visionary, human-centric
Audience:
	•	Founders, tech leaders, and startup builders
	•	Developers evolving into leaders
	•	Innovators exploring the intersection of AI and ethics
	•	Business readers seeking modern leadership philosophy

Core Premise:

True innovation begins not in lines of code but in lines of character.
“Beyond Code” explores how Soft Pyramid, founded in Lahore in 2013 by Fakhar Zaman Khan, rose from a small development studio into a global movement in AI-powered leadership — proving that empathy, trust, and learning are the real architectures of technology.

Back-Cover Synopsis

From a single laptop in Lahore to a network of AI innovators across continents, Beyond Code tells the true story of how one founder transformed a small software studio into a global philosophy of intelligent leadership.

Blending memoir, management, and mindfulness, Fakhar Zaman Khan shares lessons from a decade of building trust systems, leading human-AI teams, and redefining what it means to innovate ethically.

This is not a book about programming.
It is a book about becoming — about the people, principles, and purpose that take us beyond the screen, beyond automation, and beyond code.
